# O*NET data attribution

CareerOS includes these compressed O*NET Database 31.0 CSV tables:

- Occupation Data
- Task Statements
- Transferable Skills
- Related Occupations
- Job Zones

Original source: [O*NET Resource Center, Database 31.0](https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html)

The O*NET 31.0 Database is sponsored by the U.S. Department of Labor,
Employment and Training Administration, and developed by the National Center
for O*NET Development. The database is licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
CareerOS provides this attribution and identifies its locally derived ranking
and explanations as CareerOS transformations. Refresh the bundled tables from
the source when updating the O*NET database version.
